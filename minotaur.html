Happy Birthday Ivonne

Minotaur

As a single bead of wine
Trickles across mosaic lines
I traverse anfractuous streets
Labyrinthine, except
Time unravels like golden twine
Behooving me to be bullish
My champagne bottle, swinging Clublike.
It's eleven thirty nine.

My grin in the thought of our sinning
Curves to match the hornshaped moon.
To the palatial hotel of fine china vases
Lavish destruction begins in your room.
