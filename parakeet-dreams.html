Happy Birthday Ivonne

By Finbarre Snarey Published on November 14, 2026

Parakeet Dreams

Barcelona broke the afternoon
Into a thousand coloured shards
And laid them across the beach.

You stole pieces of sunlight
Carried them in your laughter.
While the Mediterranean, restless,
Counted our miles in waves.

When the city awakens
A thousand eyes in the trees
We will return again
To make the sunlight whole.
