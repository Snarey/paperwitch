Happy Birthday Ivonne

Gift

I hastened past the graveyard
To receive a witch's package
From the other side of the world
As a black cat haughtily watched
I gently held a brown paper parcel
Enscriped in unmistakable cursive
Home, upon the bed, I tore the wrapper
Slit the bindings and opened the box
Prepared for the sweet odour of incense
Fearsome sigils, or beetle carapaces
Most masterfully arranged
Except my gaze fell upon Tokidoki stickers
A tiny new panda and a dinosaur too
Strawberry Koalas and Caramel Corn Ruffles and Cheetos and Fritos, oh my
Treats and sweets galore were inside
From the Wonderful Witch of the West.
